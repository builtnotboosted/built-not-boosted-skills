# Worked example: a finished brand profile

A fictional business, filled at about the right level of detail. Short lines, real decisions, TODOs left honest. Use it to calibrate, not to copy.

---

```markdown
# Brand profile: Kettle & Pike Physio

Last updated: 2026-11-08

## Identity
- People: Sam and Priya (first names only in content). Both physiotherapists, both appear on camera.
- Business: Kettle & Pike Physio, a two-room clinic. Priya also runs a weekend running club, which stays separate (it has its own accounts).
- What we do (one line): We help active people over 40 get back to the thing they love after an injury, without months of guesswork.
- Location: a market town in North Yorkshire; we mention the town and nearby villages.

## Audience
- Primary: people in their 40s to 60s who run, walk, cycle or play golf, nursing a niggle they've ignored for too long.
- Secondary: desk workers with back and neck pain who think physio is "for athletes".
- What they're worried about: being told to stop doing the thing they love; wasting money on sessions that don't help; that it's "just age".
- Words they use: "my knee's gone again", "bad back", "I've pulled something", not "patellofemoral" or "lumbar".

## Offer & goal
- Offer: assessment and treatment sessions in clinic; a six-week return-to-running plan.
- An enquiry looks like: a booking through the online diary, or a phone call to the clinic.
- Main goal right now: more first-time bookings from within 15 miles.
- Prices we can mention: initial assessment price is fine in content; package prices only on the website.

## Voice
- Traits:
  - Reassuring, which means we name the fear before we explain the fix.
  - Plain-speaking, which means everyday words and one idea per post; any clinical term gets explained in brackets.
  - Lightly funny, which means a self-deprecating aside now and then, never jokes about anyone's pain.
  - Practical, which means every post leaves the reader with one thing they can try or check.
- Avoid: "game-changer", "journey", "pain-free guaranteed", "miracle", "fix you".
- Humour: light, roughly one aside a post at most. Sam's running disasters are fair game.
- Spelling locale: UK English.
- Person (I / we): "we" on the clinic accounts; "I" on Sam's and Priya's own LinkedIn.

## Platforms
| Platform | Handle / URL | Voice | Posting route | Links allowed? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook Page | /kettleandpikephysio | we | scheduler | rationed, see allow-list |
| Instagram | @kettleandpike | we | scheduler | no (link in bio) |
| Google Business Profile | Kettle & Pike Physio | we | scheduler | button only |
| LinkedIn (Sam) | /in/sam-example | I | manual | allow-list only |
| YouTube | @kettleandpike | we | manual | yes, description |

Link allow-list:
- A new blog article or YouTube video release.
- A workshop or event that asks people to book.
- Facebook: two link posts a month, on the strongest pieces.

## Content pillars
| Pillar | Share | What goes in it | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Myths and mistakes | 30% | the things people believe that keep them injured | "Rest it and it'll go away" |
| One thing to try | 25% | a safe, simple check or exercise | the stair test for knee pain |
| Behind the clinic | 20% | how a session actually works, the team, the kit | what happens in your first 20 minutes |
| Local and community | 15% | parkrun, local clubs, events | a shout-out to the Saturday parkrun volunteers |
| The offer | 10% | bookings, the return-to-running plan | new evening appointments |

## Boundaries
- Never cover: diagnosing someone in the comments; the running club (own accounts); politics; other clinics.
- Never claim: cures, guaranteed results, "pain-free", timescales for recovery, anything we can't evidence.
- Never name without consent: patients (in words or pictures), children at events.
- Always disclose: when we're paid or gifted to mention a product.

## Compliance
- Regulated: yes. Physiotherapists are registered with their professional regulator, and health claims in ads fall under UK advertising rules.
- Who signs off: Sam, for anything that mentions a treatment outcome.
- Disclaimer tiers:
  - Tier 0 (none): behind the scenes, community, team posts.
  - Tier 1 (short): "General information only, not a diagnosis. If pain persists, get it assessed." Used on any exercise or self-check post.
  - Tier 2 (full): TODO: confirm with compliance. Needed only if we ever promote a specific treatment's results.
  - Platform variants: same short line everywhere; on Google Business add nothing beyond it.
- Affiliate / ad labelling: the `writing` skill's default rule (Ad on the first line, honest price, commission disclosure).
- Reviews & testimonials: quoted verbatim from Google reviews only, first name and initial, and only where the reviewer hasn't asked to stay anonymous. Never edited, never invented.

## CTA & contact
- Ask style: a genuine question about their experience ("what's the niggle you keep ignoring?"), or a plain "book in if you'd like us to take a look".
- Bio link points to: the online booking page.
- Contact block:
  - Default: Kettle & Pike Physio · TODO: clinic phone · bookings via the link on our profile
  - Facebook: "Book via the button on our Page."
  - Instagram: "Book through the link in our bio."
  - LinkedIn: clinic name and phone, no link unless on the allow-list.

## Visual identity
| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Kettle Teal | #1F6F78 | headline highlight, buttons |
| Base | Ink | #1A1A1A | text on light |
| Light | Chalk | #F4F1EA | backgrounds |
| Accent | Pike Orange | #E07A2E | small accents only, never body text |

- Fonts: headlines in a bold condensed sans (free Google Font), body in a plain sans.
- Logo usage: small, bottom corner, never over a face.
- Image style: real-looking clinic and outdoor scenes, natural light, people mid-movement. No skeletons, no anatomical diagrams on the feed.

## Likeness
- Reference images: brand/reference-images/sam-clinic.jpg, sam-outdoors-colour.jpg, priya-headshot-colour.jpg, priya-running.jpg.
- Must match: Sam, short dark curly hair, beard, broad build, late 40s. Priya, long black hair usually tied back, slim, early 40s, no glasses.
- Rules: references only, never posted. If a generated face isn't genuinely them, the image is rebuilt without faces or held. No patients in generated images.

## Tools
- Scheduler: Publer
- Image model: Gemini image
- Video tools: phone, ffmpeg and Whisper for captions
- Analytics sources: the scheduler's analytics, Google Business Profile insights
- Other: none
```
